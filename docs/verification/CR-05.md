# CR-05 Verification Record

- Batch: CR-05 - Reports, accountant workflow, and audit readiness
- PRD references: CR-RPT-001 through CR-RPT-006; CR-05
- Status: Implementation complete; automated and rendered-output evidence recorded below
- Date: 2026-08-08

## Scope delivered

- Reports opens with six business-facing report families: management pack,
  invoice register, obligations and settlements, supplier analysis, exceptions,
  and audit trail. The detailed builder remains available below the catalog.
- RPT-006 is no longer a placeholder cash calendar. Its second immutable
  built-in version reconciles approved total, settlement, write-off, remaining
  balance, obligation state, source document, and audit-event count.
- Report inputs now come from the CR-03 approved operational register and
  deterministic obligation balance rather than the older staging snapshot.
- Every frozen contract includes source/included/excluded counts, currencies,
  exclusions, missing sources, report author and role, and an inclusive period
  boundary before export.
- Approved records carry document ID, original filename, SHA-256, and audit-event
  count into the frozen evidence contract. Reviewers can generate, reopen,
  trace, and export runs without Owner permissions.
- Saving repeatable definitions or reminder schedules is limited to Owner and
  Finance Operator. Reminders are append-only versions in schema 13.
- The management pack now starts with an executive summary and reconciliation,
  then separates obligations, supplier movement, exceptions, actions, further
  questions, and caveats across five print pages.
- CSV includes report boundary, source coverage, preflight counts, currencies,
  period boundary, and report author. Monetary cells export as exact decimal
  strings and formula-like text is neutralized.
- PDF tables wrap long names, display exact decimal money, use horizontal ranked
  bars, repeat headers, and retain frozen-run identity on every page.

## Deterministic and audit-safety evidence

- Preview persists one canonical JSON contract and SHA-256. CSV and PDF read the
  persisted contract and never query current finance data during export.
- Historical runs, exports, source hashes, reminders, and report definitions are
  append-only. A new generation creates a new report ID and does not mutate the
  prior run.
- Currency groups are never combined and no foreign-exchange conversion exists.
- Settlement availability is based on effective local events; absent settlement
  evidence is disclosed instead of interpreted as a paid or zero balance.
- Every export states that AIFOS is management support, not statutory books, a
  tax return, a bank instruction, or a payment authorisation.

## Boundaries retained

- XLSX remains excluded until dependency licensing, formula safety, and workbook
  semantics receive a separate approved gate.
- Reports do not file tax, post to an accounting ledger, initiate payments, or
  claim bank connectivity.
- Public Authenticode signing, purchaser licensing, clean-machine customer
  acceptance, and representative accountant sign-off remain CR-07 and CR-10
  controlled gates.
