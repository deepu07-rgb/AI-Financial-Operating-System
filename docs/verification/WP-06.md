  new-bank, jurisdiction tax, cash-gap rules fail closed as awaiting trusted input.

  versioned dashboard views.

  duplicate groups, review backlog, and freshness queries.

  updates, event actions, and persisted workspace filters.

  period/party/category/status/confidence filters, freshness/coverage, rule
  management, and rule-event actions.

  category trend, and price alerts, with local PNG export and accessible text.
