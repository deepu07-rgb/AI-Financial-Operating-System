# CR-04 Verification Record

- Batch: CR-04 - Manufacturer dashboard, rules, and payment priority
- PRD references: CR-FIN-001 through CR-FIN-006; CR-04
- Status: Implementation complete; repository and native smoke evidence recorded below
- Date: 2026-08-07

## Scope delivered

- Home now opens with up to three supported actions rather than decorative
  charts. Payment priorities and high-severity deterministic exceptions open
  their rule evidence directly.
- KPI cards use the CR-03 approved operational register and exact remaining
  obligation balance. Open payables, overdue exposure, due-30 exposure, open
  receivables, and exceptions drill into their supporting record IDs.
- Aging, supplier concentration, purchase trend, due exposure, cash pressure,
  exceptions, and price-variance events have record drill-down. Cash pressure
  uses a dated manual snapshot plus explicit inflow/outflow/reserve buckets and
  never claims a bank connection.
- Payment-priority rule version 1 exposes due date, remaining amount, settlement
  state, criticality, payment terms, confirmed penalty evidence, estimated
  penalty, every score component, every missing input, and affected invoice.
  Disputed obligations are excluded; ranks restart independently per currency.
- Overdue/aging, penalty, bank-change, tax-label, cash-gap, and price-variance
  coverage is shown as complete, partial, or unavailable with an exact missing
  source. Approval is never used as paid/unpaid evidence.
- Owner/CFO and payables built-in views are installed during migration. Local
  users can save audited named views. Views alter only record scope; mandatory
  currency, freshness, missing-source, and no-transfer disclosures remain.
- Supplier criticality and penalty inputs require an Owner or Finance operator,
  a bounded reason, and optional reviewed agreement evidence. Each correction
  is an append-only version and audit event.
- The synthetic sample includes a reviewed critical supplier profile so the
  explanation path is useful immediately after recreating the sample.

## Calculation and data-safety evidence

- Money remains integer minor units through storage, rules, cards, charts, and
  priority decisions. Accrued penalty uses checked integer arithmetic.
- No FX rate or conversion source exists. All totals and ranks are separated by
  currency and every view displays the limitation.
- Period filters exclude records without an invoice date instead of silently
  including them in a dated range. Old rule events are limited to invoices in
  the current filtered view.
- Operational invoices and obligation events load in set-based passes. The
  prior per-invoice obligation query was removed from the 50k path.
- Schema 12 adds only append-only supplier finance profiles and newer built-in
  dashboard-view versions. Migration history upgrades from every released
  schema and detects checksum drift.

## Boundaries retained

- AIFOS cannot initiate, authorize, schedule, or confirm a bank transfer.
- There is no API key, provider SDK, live AI model, live bank feed, accounting
  credential, network connector, or FX source in CR-04.
- Bank-change detection remains unavailable until two reviewed, dated bank
  fingerprints can be compared. A single trusted masked fingerprint is not
  presented as change history.
- CR-05 owns management packs, accountant workflow, and frozen output polish.
  Public installer signing and clean-machine release acceptance remain later
  controlled gates.
